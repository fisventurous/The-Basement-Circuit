      A WORD FROM THE PROMOTER

      How to play

      Any kind of contender can enter. Your line is a starting point, not a character template.

      1. 01

        Ask around. Pete has specific tells and quirks. Put the counter in your description to turn his weakness against him.

      2. 02

        Write one line. Invent anyone you like. The AI inks vivid details and signed moves while keeping the spirit of your idea.

      3. 03

        Three-bout ticket. Test your contender across a 3-bout series. Returning to your corner sponges down your fighter, fully restoring their HP and resetting fatigue before the next bout begins.

      4. 04

        Watch the tape. Each bout runs up to seven rounds or a knockout. Share the link or check your scrapbook records.

      Practice cards use local rules. Connect Pollinations to ink with AI; each card uses one text call.
